Life Helper Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Life Helper Bot is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single individual (Andrew McDonough) for his own private use. It is not a public or multi-user product, and it is not available for anyone else to install or use.

What this app does

Life Helper Bot connects to the owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail accounts to help manage scheduling, tasks, and email drafts through a private Telegram chat interface that only the owner can access.

What data it accesses

  • Google Calendar — reads and creates/updates calendar events on the owner's own calendar, to help manage scheduling.
  • Gmail — reads, creates, and sends email drafts on the owner's own behalf. The app never reads or accesses email outside of drafts it creates, and any sending of an email requires the owner's explicit confirmation first.

How data is stored and used

All application data (tasks, reminders, and meal-planning notes) is stored locally on the owner's own personal computer, in a local database file. Nothing is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party other than the underlying service providers required to operate the app: Google (Calendar/Gmail APIs), Telegram (chat interface), and Anthropic (the Claude API, which powers the app's reasoning).

No data collected through this app is used for advertising, and no data is shared with any third party for their own purposes.

Data retention and deletion

Because this is a single-user personal tool, data is retained only for as long as the owner continues to use the app. The owner can revoke this app's access to their Google account at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party access, which immediately and permanently revokes all access.
